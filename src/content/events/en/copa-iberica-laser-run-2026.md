# Iberian Laser-Run Cup 2026

The Iberian Laser-Run Cup 2026 will take place on December 5th, 2026, in Valladolid, Spain.

## General Information

- **Date:** December 5th, 2026
- **Location:** Valladolid, Spain
- **Type:** Competition
- **Event:** Laser-Run

## About the Competition

The Iberian Laser-Run Cup brings together athletes from Spain and Portugal to compete in this Modern Pentathlon discipline. It's a great opportunity to test our training progress and enjoy a unique competitive experience.

## Registration

More information and registration details are available through the International Modern Pentathlon Union (UIPM).

We encourage all members of the 5 en 1 club to take part!
